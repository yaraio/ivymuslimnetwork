ABOUT OUR ORGANIZATION

Cultivating the next generation
of Muslim leaders.

Ivy Muslim Network connects Muslim high school students with university students for mentorship, guidance, and community as they prepare for higher education.

Our mission

We help students navigate the college application journey with confidence, while staying true to their experiences, ambitions, and faith.

How we support students

  • Essay and personal statement guidance
  • One-to-one mentorship
  • Application strategy and college list building
  • Interview preparation
  • Guidance on finding a passion project

Our university references identify institutions of interest to our community. They do not imply university endorsement or formal partnership.
